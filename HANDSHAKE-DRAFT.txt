Title: King Kitty

Description (414/500 characters, entered in Handshake but not published):
King Kitty is a 2D multiplayer alley race. Everyone starts together and runs automatically. Jump over obstacles and grab birds or mice for small, random speed boosts. Obstacles slow you down but never eliminate you. The first player across the finish line wins, with final times shown for everyone. The pixel-art alley scrolls in layers, with separate character animations, moving scenery, and interactive objects.
